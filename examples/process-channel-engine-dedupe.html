Workflow

Target Account Deduplication

Open in HubSpot arrow_forward
play_arrow

Starts

When a company is created.

skip_next

Skips

Companies that already have an HG child company ID.

repeat_one

Runs

Once. It will not enroll the same company again.

Detect

Same root domain

The part of the website before the country ending. cocacola.com and cocacola.co.uk are a match. No website → the workflow ends. Name and LinkedIn are not used.

Found a twin. Merge it?

Merge

We are sure

  • Record Source is not CRM UI. Nobody typed this in by hand.
  • Lifecycle is Target or Lead

Which company we keep

  1. Target Account is true
  2. Most contacts
  3. The website ends in .com
  4. Older HubSpot record

After the merge

  • If either record is a Customer, the leftover record stays a Customer
  • Same idea for a churned customer
  • A short note is written on the winner

Task

No auto-merge

HubSpot makes a task for the company owner. Due in three weekdays. Nothing is merged.

  • Record Source is CRM UI. A person created this in HubSpot.
  • Lifecycle is MQL, SQL, Opportunity, Customer, or Live

Any other lifecycle (Subscriber, blank, and so on) just ends. No merge. No task.

Workflow

Contact Deduplication

Open in HubSpot arrow_forward
play_arrow

Starts

When a contact is created.

skip_next

Skips

No extra skip rule. Every new contact can enroll.

repeat_one

Runs

Once. Same four-minute wait as companies.

Do these two contacts look like the same person?

Merge

We are sure

If one of the following is true:

  • Same email
  • Same name AND same company. One email can be blank. We keep the contact that has the email.
  • Same name AND LinkedIn (one contact not associated to deal or company)
  • Same name OR same LinkedIn, AND only one is tied to a company. We keep the contact with the company.

Winners selected by:

  1. Has an email. A contact with no email loses.
  2. Lower bounce count. Empty or under 1 beats a bouncing inbox.
  3. Tied to a company
  4. Fuller profile: name, title, or owner filled in
  5. More recent activity
  6. Older HubSpot record

After the merge

  • Customer stage is protected, same as companies
  • A short note is written on the winner

Stop

Workflow stops

Nothing is merged. HubSpot checks whether the contact has an owner, then ends. No task is created. Open the contact record to review.

  • The only clue is a phone number
  • The match is weak
  • Both are Customers, but with different relationship labels
