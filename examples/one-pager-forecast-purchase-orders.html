Materials Contract forecasting

This is a Recommendation

Forecast Purchase Orders in HubSpot

Stop importing forecast spreadsheets. Give sales a table on the Materials Contract, and let HubSpot create the Purchase Order records.

Bottom line

Create a dedicated Purchase Order custom object for forecast records and manage those forecasts directly from the Materials Contract. Reps enter many future orders in one sitting. HubSpot creates the records and associations. No import file, no hunting record IDs, no association workflows.

Replace Entire Forecast

Use this when a new projection lands and the old forecast should come off the board.

Replace Entire Forecast would:

1

Find the current forecast

Load Purchase Orders associated to this Materials Contract where PO Type is Forecast.

2

Archive the old lines

Remove or archive those forecast records. Actual Purchase Orders stay put.

3

Create the new forecast

Write one Purchase Order record for each row in the table.

4

Associate to the contract

Attach the new records to the same Materials Contract. No ID hunt.

Why this matters

Today, a new forecast means finding and deleting last quarter’s imported rows by hand. Replace Entire Forecast is that cleanup, without the scavenger hunt.
