Week 1: Start Small, Target Engaged Users

  • Send to your most engaged contacts first (those who open & click regularly).
  • Start with 500–1,000 emails per day, then gradually increase.
  • Split across different providers (Gmail, Yahoo, Outlook, etc.).
  • Avoid promotional-heavy language—keep it simple & valuable.

Day 4: Don’t forget to test

  • Testing helps you identify gaps.
  • Avoid common pitfalls.

Week 2: Increase Daily Volume by 2x

  • Expand to slightly less engaged contacts, but still warm leads.
  • Increase daily sends to 2,000–5,000 (but only if open rates stay good).
  • Keep monitoring bounces, spam complaints, and unsubscribes (if they spike, slow down).

Week 3-4: Keep Scaling Steadily

  • Slowly add more of your list, including newer or less engaged contacts.
  • Aim for 5,000–20,000 per day by the end of the month.
  • Keep your open rate above 15% (below that = providers get suspicious).
  • If anything tanks (deliverability drops, spam complaints rise), pause and adjust.

Post-Warm-up (Full Scale Sending)

  • By week 4+, you should be able to send at full volume if engagement metrics look good.
  • If you hit 100,000+ emails per month, consider segmenting further to maintain deliverability.

Extra Tips to Stay Out of Spam

  • Authenticate Your Domain – Set up SPF, DKIM, and DMARC. Learn DMARC is a great tool for troubleshooting any errors with auth.
  • Send Valuable Emails – No spammy language ("FREE MONEY!" = instant blacklist).
  • Keep Lists Clean – Don’t buy lists. Only email people who actually signed up.
  • Monitor Your Reputation – Use tools like Google Postmaster Tools and MXToolbox to track sender reputation.
  • Stay Consistent – Don’t send 100K one week and then nothing for a month. Providers like stability.
